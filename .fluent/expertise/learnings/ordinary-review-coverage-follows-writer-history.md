name: ordinary-review-coverage-follows-writer-history
description: Share effective ordinary review coverage across advancement and completed recovery while preserving scoped evidence and Writer history
metadata:
  type: architecture
---

For code-producing Attempts, `recovery_contracts::review_coverage` derives
ordinary review coverage from completed Tasks, their candidate contexts, and
saved verdicts. The Attempt loop and completed recovery consume this shared
read-only selection; the neutral contract does not depend on the executor.
Every built-in role needs a passing ordinary review before final Tester or
Learning. A targeted evidence review credits only its declared evidence scope;
it cannot supply missing ordinary coverage or replace a failed ordinary verdict.
After targeted acceptance, schedule any missing ordinary role through native
review planning while retaining the targeted Task and its evidence.

Carry a passing ordinary review forward only while its context is valid and
every subsequent completed Writer delta leaves its domain unchanged. Comparing
only the latest pair of Writers can lose an earlier invalidation; comparing
only the original and final trees can hide a change followed by a revert.
Unknown paths or unverifiable comparisons invalidate every domain. A replacement
review names its actual candidate without rewriting historical Task contexts.

Apply the same coverage assessment during planning, review interpretation,
saved final-Tester admission, unfinished pre-Learning resume, and completed
Approach-recovery validation on foreground and queue routes. A later ordinary
Review replaces its domain's effective verdict while earlier failures remain
immutable history. Requiring every historical Review after the latest Writer
to pass would reject a completed recovery that advancement accepted.

Keep targeted acceptance and canonical verification separate from ordinary
coverage. Completed recovery still validates Plan receipts, current required
checks, candidate-bound final Tester and gate evidence, and the Learning
handoff. Repair missing coverage without manufacturing another Writer. Preserve
already accepted Learning when repeated resume observes its expertise commit.

Lifecycle fixtures that begin at Tester or Learning must establish ordinary
review prerequisites. Recovery regressions should let public resume schedule
the missing roles; inserting those roles by hand would conceal a planning gap.
Continue composed recovery regressions through successful Learning and another
public resume. Retain an earlier failed ordinary Review, accept its requested
proof through targeted review, and obtain a fresh ordinary pass. Then change
the effective ordinary report, targeted acceptance, and canonical Tester result
independently; each invalid case must reject foreground and queue resume
without dispatching workers or changing protected state.
